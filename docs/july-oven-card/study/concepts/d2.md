# d2 · Still Glass — the budget-first concept

## Idea

**Still Glass** begins with the budget and adds no features to it. Each state has one job line and the same four fixed slots:
- **who / what** (name + status mark) at the top-left;
- **the number** (one huge thin condensed numeral, with a tiny honest qualifier under it, as on the oven);
- **the window** (the camera, uncropped 4:3, in the same right-hand place in every running state);
- **the hand** (one action in a fixed bottom-right spot: Stop, or Turn off when done).

The only extra is a quiet group of +1/+5/+10, and only while a timer runs. The idle home is the oven's own first screen: a centred clock and date, and a row of glowing square tiles in three pages with dots.

"Glass" comes from four things, not from extra elements:
- **Surface:** near-black with one diagonal specular sheen, like the reflection on the oven door.
- **Light:** only heat glows. The *heat line* is a single progress line drawn like the tile glyph's glowing element. It warms from blue to ember while preheating, stays ember while cooking, and goes dark and dashed when heat stops (door open, offline).
- **Type:** a thin condensed numeral.
- **Restraint:** no dividers, no chips at rest, no second paging system.

In Match-HA mode it becomes a normal HA card with the same slots, so it sits naturally among other HA cards.

## 2. Layouts per size

Card height = rows×56 + (rows−1)×8. All inner measures are computed from the card box with container-query units (`cqw`/`cqh` on `.d2-pane`, `container-type:size`). Grids use `minmax(0,1fr)` / `min-height:0`. Every state fits at section widths 358 and 500 (checked with the harness fit checker, which reports "All cards fit their boxes" in all three looks). The 320 px floor was checked by arithmetic below.

### Standard — 12 × 6 (376 px tall)
Grid: 2 columns (`1fr | camw`), 3 rows (`auto | 1fr | auto`), padding 14 / 16, column gap 16, row gap 12.

| Measure | 500 px | 358 px | 320 px |
|---|---|---|---|
| Camera window `camw = 34cqw` (4:3) | 170 × 127 | 122 × 91 | 109 × 82 |
| Hero size `min(26cqw, 29cqh)` | 109 px ("12:30" ≈ 200 px wide in a 274 px column) | 93 px (≈ 170 in 188) | 83 px (≈ 150 in 163) |
| Hero text (Done) `min(9.5cqw,14cqh)` | 47 px | 34 px | 30 px |
| Stop `max(96px, camw)` × 48 | 170 × 48 | 122 × 48 | 109 × 48 |
| Chips 3 × 48 × 48, gap 6 → gap to Stop | 142 px | **48 px** (one target width) | 35 px (see deviations) |
| Left stack height (hero + qualifier + line + 2 lines) | ≈ 184 of 236 available | ≈ 170 (+20 if line B wraps) of 236 | fits |

Idle (Off):
- Top row is `1fr auto 1fr`: name + status on the left, the centred clock `min(15cqw,19cqh)` (71 px at 500, 54 px at 358) with the date under it, and the camera-off window `max(64px,16cqw)` wide on the right (80 × 60 at 500, 64 × 48 at 358).
- Pager: five tiles per page, `tile = min((100cqw − 32 − 4·gap)/5, 88px)`, `gap = clamp(10px,3.4cqw,20px)`. That gives tiles of 80 px at 500, 55 px at 358 and 49 px at 320, all ≥ 48.
- Dot row: 48 px tall, each dot a 48 × 48 button.

### Wall tablet — 24 × 9, spanning 2 section columns (1032 × 568 px at a 500 base; 748 × 568 at 358)
- The same slots with the same positions, scaled up. Padding 26 / 28, column gap 40.
- Hero `min(15cqw, 25cqh)`: **142 px** (brief range 110–150). Status 19 px caps, projection 26 px.
- Camera `30cqw`: 310 × 232 (224 × 168 at 748).
- Stop 64 px tall × camera width. Chips 76 × 64.
- **The one extra layer:** a word-sized oven-temperature sparkline with amber door marks from real door data, in the running states that have history (cooking, probe, door open). It sits at the foot of the left column and flexes into the leftover height (18–84 px). Tapping it opens detail › Graph. It is a button, never a page.
- Idle wall: clock `min(12cqw,19cqh)` = 108 px in *secondary* colour, tiles 132 px, labels 19 px, dots 56 px. The glyph glow is off at rest (dim idle panel) and returns on hover or focus.

### Compact — 12 × 1 (56 px)
`[camera 64×48] [hero 31 px] [status mark + word / projection] … [action 48 px tall]`
- Live frames carry a tiny "LIVE" pill on the thumbnail. Old frames show "2 min" / "3 min" in amber.
- The Off row is `[camera-off 64×48] [clock] [○ OFF / Kitchen] [Start…]`. "Start…" opens the start flow and never heats.
- Width at 358: 4 + 64 + 10 + ~56 + 10 + meta ≥ 80 + 10 + ~84 + 4. The meta column ellipsises before anything spills.
- Done at 358: the hero "Take food out" (20 px, ≈ 95 px) leaves ≈ 70 px for "✓ DONE / 2 min ago".

## 3. Idle home and Settings
- **Page order is the oven's order:** Bake · Roast · Reheat · Broil · Toast | Air fry · Grill · Proof · Pizza · Dehydrate | Slow cook · Keep warm · **Settings**. Bake and Roast, the modes proven for remote start, are first. There are 3 pages (the r2 cap), and the same 5-per-page split at every width, so spatial memory holds across phone and wall.
- **Tiles** are square (the lead's SHOULD), with a 22 % corner radius:
  - dark ember glass body (#1D1210 → #2E1511);
  - a warm floor bloom;
  - a thin ember rim;
  - one diagonal sheen;
  - the glyph draws the heating element(s) and glows from inside (r1 §2).

  Labels sit below, in sentence case. The column gap equals the horizontal gap in every row. With one row per page, "equal both ways" reduces to equal horizontal gaps.
- **Settings tile:** it is **the same rounded-square shape** as the mode tiles (the lead's ask), so the grid rhythm is unbroken. It uses **neutral grey glass and a grey gear**, as the oven's Settings item does (r1 §2: utilities are grey, not ember), so heat colour stays reserved for heat.
- **Paging:**
  - swipe (scroll-snap);
  - the tap alternative is three 48 × 48 dot buttons with "Show page n of 3" labels;
  - keyboard: dots are buttons.

  Scrolling is instant, with no smooth scrolling or animation. Settings is also reachable from the detail view's Settings tab, so the pager is never the only path.
- **No hint text** ("swipe for more" etc.).

## 4. Quick add and the temperature graph
- **+1 / +5 / +10:**
  - They appear only when a timer is running (cooking with timer).
  - Outline chips, 48 × 48, left-aligned.
  - Stop is filled, right-aligned, fixed in place across all states.
  - The gap between the groups is ≥ one target width at 358 and 500.
  - A small "min" suffix appears when there is room (≥ 420 px).
  - They are not on compact, and not in door-open or ready (no timer running). They are repeated inside the Timer row in detail.
- **Graph:** detail › Graph, full width:
  - oven line (ember) and food line (blue), so the two differ in hue family, not only in lightness;
  - dashed target lines at 400 and 145 ("145 goal");
  - door-open periods as amber bands labelled "Door", drawn only from real door intervals;
  - direct end labels plus a legend;
  - a one-sentence plain-language summary under it ("The door was open once, for 1 min 24 s at 13 min…"), which doubles as the screen-reader text.

  Each past cook in History opens its own graph. On the card, only the wall size shows the sparkline (see above).

## 5. Several ovens
One card per oven stays the default (r2 §3d). The optional multi-oven card is a **focus + rows** switcher, 12 × (6 + ovens − 1):
- **Focus:** the first heating oven, shown as a full standard pane.
- **Every other heating oven:** a compact row under it with name, hero, status word and its own Stop. It is never hidden.
- **Ovens that are off:** a quiet outline pill "○ Kitchen Off" with no colour or highlight.
- Tapping a row or pill makes it the focus.
- With more than one oven, every action names its oven: "Stop / Kitchen", "Stop / Garage" as a two-line label on the button, and aria-labels such as "Add 5 minutes to Kitchen".

This is not a second paging system: all ovens are visible at once.

## 6. Detail view (HA more-info dialog, ~420 px)
Header: close, name, status mark. Tabs: **Now · Graph · History · Settings**. The flows below are two levels at most (NN/g).
- **Now:**
  - large 4:3 camera with its age, and Watch video · 5 min;
  - hero + projection + heat line;
  - rows: *Oven* 400 °F (− / +), *Timer* 12:30 left (+1 / +5 / +10), *Food probe target* 145 °F (− / +) with "Food is 128 °F now";
  - command acknowledgement line;
  - full-width **Stop cooking**.
- **Graph:** as above.
- **History:** past cooks, each with its last camera picture, mode, temperature, date, duration and food temperature. Tap a cook to see its graph.
- **Settings:**
  - Alerts: toggles for the five events.
  - Camera: turn on when cooking starts; keep on for 10 min after done.
  - Remote start: read-only "Allowed for Bake and Roast — change it on the oven: Settings › App permissions".
  - Oven: name, model, software, connection and last update.
- **Start (reached from a tile):**
  - Step 1 is the mode's settings.
  - Step 2 is the review shown here: oven, temperature, timer, preheat.
  - One ember-gradient **"Start baking on Kitchen"** button. This is the only ember button in the whole system, because it is the one that makes heat.
  - "Change settings", and × to cancel.
  - "This review closes in 4:52. Nothing heats until you press Start."
  - Modes the oven does not allow remotely show "Start this on the oven" instead of the button.
  - The same two-step governs **Keep cooking** after Done.

## 8. Typeface
**Barlow** (words) and **Barlow Condensed** (numerals, 300 in dark and Match HA, 400 in light). Both are OFL and on Google Fonts. Why:
- Barlow is a slightly rounded grotesk in the DIN / highway-sign lineage, the closest open match to DIN Next's proportions and terminals.
- It ships Light Condensed and Regular in one family, so the card keeps June's "thin condensed numeral + plain-width labels" pairing with a single family.
- Numerals are tabular, so the timer does not jitter.
- Status words use the regular-width face in caps. NN/g: caps and regular width read faster in a glance, and condensed is kept for the one big number, where size compensates.

## 9. Accessibility
- **Targets:** every one is ≥ 48 px: Stop, chips, dots, tiles (≥ 49 px at 320), the camera window (64 × 48 minimum), detail steppers, the quiet oven pill. Wall targets are 56–64 px.
- **Status:** always colour + shape + word:
  - hollow ring = off;
  - half ring = preheat;
  - dot = cooking;
  - check ring = ready;
  - filled check = done;
  - triangle = door;
  - dashed crossed ring = offline.
- **Stale:** the hero dims to 42 %, the line turns grey and dashed, the frame is greyed, and the age shows in amber. Staleness is visible in the hero, not in a footnote.
- **Swipe** has the tap alternative (dots). Nothing is drag-only or long-press-only.
- **Reduced motion:** there is no motion at all. `prefers-reduced-motion` also kills transitions, and forced-colors fallbacks exist for the line and the Stop button.
- **Screen readers:**
  - the heat line is a `progressbar` with a value;
  - the camera button says "Oven camera, live. Open larger view" / "picture from 3 min ago";
  - chips say "Add 5 minutes to Kitchen";
  - Stop says "Stop the Kitchen oven";
  - the graph has a full text summary.
- **Contrast:** AA text contrast in all looks, as given in the tokens.
