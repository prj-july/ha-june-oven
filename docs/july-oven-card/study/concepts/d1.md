# d1 · Second Screen

**Idea.** The card is a second copy of the June oven's own glass screen, and next to it is a window into the oven. The screen half copies the oven's cooking screens as closely as a landscape box allows:
- one huge, thin, condensed numeral;
- inside one of the oven's two rings: the thermal-gradient ring while preheating (blue → red, filling toward the target), or the flat ember ring while cooking (draining as time runs out);
- a tiny qualifier under the numeral ("left", "or more", "paused");
- two or three small centred lines under the ring.

The window half is the camera. It is a plain 4:3 still with one small caption naming the oven and the age of the picture. It sits beside the screen because the real oven puts the cavity window and the screen side by side on the same black door. Under the two halves is a single action row that never moves. The one prominent, outcome-labelled button ("Stop", or "Finish" when done) sits bottom-right. The only quiet group (+1 / +5 / +10) sits bottom-left, and only while a timer runs.

When the oven is off, the card becomes the oven's home screen: a centred clock and date, pages of glowing element tiles with tappable dots, and the camera window shrunk to a small "Camera off · Turn on" pane in the corner.

Everything the oven screen never had to handle lives one tap away in the detail dialog: the graph, adjustments, history, settings and the start review.

## 2. Layouts and measurements

All sizes are computed from the card box. The root is a CSS size container:
- The ring is `min(column width, column height − lines)`.
- The camera is `min(column width, (column height − caption) × 4/3)`.
- Tiles are `min(width / 4, row height × 1.16)`.

Nothing depends on content height. Card height = rows × 56 + (rows − 1) × 8. I verified fit at section widths 320, 340, 358, 420 and 500, in all three looks and both HA themes, with no clipped text. Chip-to-Stop spacing is at least 48 px at every width.

- Off: "[cam-off] ○ Off / Kitchen / Camera off [Start…]". "Start…" opens the start flow; nothing heats.

Tapping the pane when the camera is off turns it on for 5 min.

## 3. Idle home and Settings

- **Header, persistent on every page:**
  - left: oven name + "○ Off";
  - centre: thin clock 46 px + grey date;
  - right: camera-off pane.

  On the oven, the clock is on page 1 only. I keep it on both pages so the camera and status don't jump (layout stability, r3 M3 card).
- **Tiles** are dark glass lit from inside:
  - the glyph is the heating element (bottom bar = Bake, top bar = Broil, fan = Air fry, …);
  - an ember bloom sits behind the lit element, with a diagonal specular sheen and a rim.
  - They are 1.16 : 1, with sentence-case labels below, as on the oven (r1 §2).
  - Equal 14 px gaps both ways between cells. Page 1 holds the 8 common modes; page 2 holds the other 4 + Settings.
- **At rest the glow is low.** Hover, focus or press raises the bloom to full. This matches "nearly invisible until you start interacting" (r1 §1, TechCrunch). Wall panels also fall back after 2 min untouched to the oven's own clock-only idle state (r1 `unk_36`: tiles hidden, clock dimmed); any tap restores them. This is specified, not drawn in the harness.
- **Dots** are real buttons, 48 × 48 each, with `aria-label` "Show page 2 of 2". Swipe is optional; tap and keyboard both work. There is only one pager on the card, only in Off, and at most 2 pages.
- **Settings is a tile** of the same shape, after the last mode. Its glyph is the oven's own grey gear disc on a neutral, non-glowing body. **Why a tile and not a circle:** the lead asked for the same shape (SHOULD), and a tile keeps the grid rhythm. The grey disc inside keeps the oven's cue that Settings is a utility, not heat (r1 §2 "warm colour only for heat"). Clean and Connect are oven-only jobs and are left out.
- **Tapping a mode** opens the start flow in the detail view. It never heats. Modes the oven does not allow remotely open a sheet saying "Broil starts on the oven" with no start button. Only Bake and Roast offer Review.

## 4. Quick add and graph

- **+1 / +5 / +10** are quiet outline pills in the bottom-left. They show only while a timer exists (Cooking, Door open) and in Done, where they act as June's "Keep baking".
  - They are separated from Stop by **space** (≥ 48 px, measured), **style** (outline vs filled red), and **alignment** (left vs right). Stop's position never changes across states.
  - An `aria-label` names the outcome and oven, e.g. "Add 5 minutes" / "Keep baking 5 minutes", with the group labelled "Add time, Kitchen".
- **Graph** is one tap away (detail › Graph), following every comparable (r2 §3b, r3). It shows:
  - the oven line in solid ember and the food line in dashed teal, so the two differ in dash as well as colour;
  - both targets as dotted reference lines;
  - door-open intervals as amber bands with a "Door" label, taken **only from door data**;
  - legend buttons that toggle each series (Combustion / ThermoWorks);
  - one plain sentence underneath: "Door opened once at 13 min for 1 min 24 s. Heating paused; the oven fell to 352° and was back at 400° by 16 min."

  There is no sparkline on the card. The ring already answers the glance question, and a sparkline would be a sixth element type.

## 5. Several ovens

- The card shows the **selected** oven in the standard layout; by default this is the first oven that is heating.
- Every **other heating oven** appears above it as a compact strip: camera thumbnail, hero, name, status, and its own **"Stop Garage"**. It is never hidden and needs no tab.
- **Off ovens** collapse to a quiet 48 px row, "○ Kitchen · Off  Show ›", with no colour and no highlight.
- Tapping a strip or row makes that oven the selected one; the strips themselves are the switcher, so there is no extra paging system.
- With more than one oven, every action names its oven: "Stop Kitchen", "Stop Garage", camera caption "Kitchen · Live", and the review button says "in Kitchen".
- Grid: 12 × 7 for two ovens (56 px strip + the 376 px standard), plus one row per extra heating oven.
- Separate cards per oven remain the HA default.

## 6. Detail view (more-info style, 420 px)

- **Tabs:** Now · Graph · History · Settings. Start and Review are sheets opened from tiles. All targets are ≥ 48 px and there are two levels at most.
  - **Now:** ring + lines header; full-width camera still with "Watch live · 5 min"; Adjust rows (oven temperature − / + stepper, timer with +1 +5 +10, food target stepper); full-width "Stop baking".
  - **Graph:** as §4.
  - **History:** past cooks, each with its last still, mode, time, duration and door count, opening that cook's graph and time-lapse.
  - **Settings (in-card):**
    - Alerts: Ready and done; Food reached target; Door left open.
    - Camera: on while cooking; after a cook, 5 min then off.
    - Remote start: shown read-only, "Allowed for Bake and Roast · Change this on the oven: Settings › App Permissions".
    - Oven info and connection.
- **Start (step 1 of 2):**
  - the mode tile, "Nothing heats until you review and start";
  - temperature and timer steppers, probe row, and a "Review" button with a separate "×" row.
- **Review (step 2 of 2):**
  - a big "400°", "Convection bake in **Kitchen** · 25 min timer";
  - "Review expires in 4:52" with a static bar that counts down the 5-minute window;
  - stacked, outcome-labelled buttons: **Start Baking** (warm gradient), Change, ×.
  - After 5 min the review closes and says "Review expired, nothing started".
- **Card editor** (not drawn): Look (Match HA / Light / Dark), Oven(s), Camera entity, Show quick add, Show clock on idle. Size comes from HA's drag handles; `getGridOptions` returns `{columns: 12, rows: 6, min_columns: 12, min_rows: 6}`, the wall preset is 24 × 9 in a two-column section, and compact is 12 × 1.
- **Integration config:** address, TLS. Not on the card.

## 8. Typeface

**Barlow Condensed** (Light 300 for numerals and clock) and **Barlow Semi Condensed** (400–600 for lines and labels), both SIL OFL on Google Fonts. Barlow was drawn from California licence plates and highway signage. It shares DIN's low-contrast, slightly rounded, engineered grotesk skeleton, with square-ish counters, flat terminals and a straight-legged "1". The Condensed Light is the closest open match to DIN Next Condensed Light on the oven's timer and clock.

I use the semi-condensed width for words because NN/g found condensed type 11 % slower for glance words (r2); the fully condensed cut is reserved for numerals, as on the oven. Fallbacks: Roboto Condensed, Arial Narrow.

## 9. Accessibility

- **Hero is the largest text** in every state: 43–68 px standard, 85–126 px wall, 32 px compact.
- **Status = word + shape + colour** throughout (↑ Preheating ember · ✓ Ready green · flame Cooking ember · ⚠ Door open amber · ✓● Take food out green · dashed ⊘ Not responding grey · ○ Off grey). The ring shape changes too: gradient, ember, dimmed, full green, dashed.
- **Targets:** chips 48–56, Stop 48–56 tall, dots 48 × 48, tiles ≥ 71 × 61, camera pane ≥ 64 × 48, steppers 48.
- **No swipe-only path:** dots, Tab / arrow keys and the wall's single grid all work without a swipe. No long-press, no drag. Steppers replace the app's tick ruler.
- **Reduced motion:** the only transitions (tile glow, dot width) are removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`. There is no pulsing and no animation frames.
- **Screen readers:**
  - the card is a group labelled with a spoken summary, e.g. "Kitchen oven: Cooking, 12 minutes left, done at 6:42 PM";
  - every button names its outcome and oven ("Stop Kitchen oven now", "Keep baking 5 minutes");
  - the graph has a text alternative plus the prose note.
- **Stale looks stale:** the hero is dimmed to 50 % (still ≥ 3 : 1 as large text) and struck through, the ring turns grey dashed, "last known" replaces the qualifier, and the camera still is desaturated with its age.
- **Wall panels dim** after 2 min to clock-only; no motion at rest.

## 11. Evidence vs. my own judgement

**Evidence-backed:**
- The two rings, the thin condensed numeral, the qualifier, the centred lines, the bold food line, element-glyph tiles, 1.16 : 1 tiles, and a grey gear for Settings (r1 §1–2, §5).
- Outcome labels "Start Baking", "Finish", "Keep baking" (r1 §1, H&G photos).
- The budget slots and ≤ 1 prominent action (r2 §2).
- Chips separated from Stop by space, style and alignment (r2 §3c, NN/g).
- Graph one tap away with legend toggles and reference lines (r2 §3b, r3 Combustion / ThermoWorks).
- Camera as a refreshing still with age, video on request (r3 Miele / Anova).
- Stale honesty by degrading the hero (r2 step 6, r3 ThermoWorks).
- Strips per heating oven with off ovens compressed (r2 §3d).
- "Put food in" / "Take food out" verbs (r3 MEATER).
- Dots only for the idle picker, ≤ 3 pages, with a tap path (r2 §3a).
- Semi-condensed type for words (NN/g in r2).
- Remote start only for Bake/Roast (brief F).

**My own judgement (flagged):**
- Placing the camera **beside** the screen as "the oven's window". It is inspired by the physical door, not by the screen.
- Keeping the clock header on both idle pages.
- No paging on wall idle.
- Keeping the chips in Door-open, and using them as "Keep baking" in Done.
- Red for Stop and green for Done. The oven's own done colour is unverified; the app has #1CA600.
- "9 min" plus "or more" as the probe hero instead of mm:ss.
- The ≥ 48 px chip–Stop gap threshold (r2 marks it [Own]).
- Capping the camera below the ring's size.
- The rest-glow reduction and the wall clock-only dim state.
- The strikethrough on stale values.
- Door marks drawn as amber bands. r3 found no product that does this, so it is new ground; it uses a single mark type.
